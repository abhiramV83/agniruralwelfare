Membership Terms and Conditions

The terms and conditions of membership are designed with you, our members, in mind. All members must keep to the same terms and conditions. They are necessary to make sure we can offer a safe platform for members to share project management knowledge, tools, resources, and experience.

1. Definitions

ARWS NGOs Board Member: members that are Board Members of ARWS NGOs. ARWS NGOs Team Member: members that are Member staff of ARWS NGOs. In these Membership Terms, “we” or “us” means ARWS NGOs and “you” or “your” means a Member.

2. Membership Benefits

Full access to the ARWS NGOs website. Access to ARWS NGOs management tools and resources. The opportunity to attend online and face to face events according to each event's rules and fees. To submit articles, tools and other forms of publications to be included in the ARWS NGOs website. ARWS NGOs reserves the right to change membership benefits at any time and may designate areas which specific member levels can access due to their content.

3. Fees and Services

Membership is free. Member can donate to ARWS NGOs, but does not receive any benefit or claim for donation to ARWS NGOs.

4. Public Forums

ARWS NGOs may make messaging services, chat services, bulletin boards, message boards, blogs and other forums available through the Site. Members must not upload, post, transmit, distribute or publish material that restricts or inhibits another user; is fraudulent, unlawful, threatening, abusive, harassing, libelous, defamatory, obscene, vulgar, offensive, pornographic, profane, sexually explicit or indecent; encourages criminal conduct or violates law; infringes third-party rights; contains a virus, spyware or harmful component; contains embedded links, commercial solicitation, advertising, chain letters, mass mailings or pyramid schemes; constitutes spam; or contains false or misleading indications of origin, endorsement or statements of fact. Members must not impersonate any person or entity. Members are responsible for information they post and its consequences. ARWS NGOs reserves the right to monitor, review, disclose, edit, refuse or remove information where necessary and may restrict access to forums for breaches of these terms or applicable law. Comments may be recorded and stored in multiple places and members should be careful about disclosing sensitive, embarrassing, proprietary or confidential information.

5. Suspension or Termination

ARWS NGOs may suspend or terminate registration and deny access to the Member Area immediately if a member breaches these Membership Terms or other Site rules; ARWS NGOs is unable to verify or authenticate information provided; or ARWS NGOs believes the member's actions may cause legal liability. By proceeding with this application you confirm that, as a member of the ARWS NGOs community, you will be committed to the development of others and intend to develop your management skills and professionalism continuously. You agree to abide by the ARWS NGOs Terms and Conditions for Membership. If accepted into membership but any information is misleading or false, membership may be revoked with immediate effect, without right of appeal. Member agrees to pay annual fees established for its Membership Class, as may be amended from time to time in accordance with the Bylaws.
